RISK ANALYSIS
================================================================================

MARKET RISKS:
1. Technology Sector Volatility: The software and cloud computing sectors
   are subject to rapid technological change. Failure to innovate could
   impact market share.

2. Customer Concentration: The top 10 customers represent 35% of revenue.
   Loss of a major customer could significantly impact results.

3. Competition: Intense competition from larger players (AWS, Microsoft Azure)
   in the cloud infrastructure space.

OPERATIONAL RISKS:
1. Talent Retention: The industry has high employee turnover. Retaining key
   engineering talent is critical.

2. Data Security: As a cloud provider, any security breach could damage
   reputation and lead to legal liability.

3. Regulatory Compliance: Increasing data protection regulations (GDPR, CCPA)
   require continuous investment in compliance infrastructure.

FINANCIAL RISKS:
1. Debt Level: Current debt-to-equity ratio of 0.76 is reasonable but
   further leverage could impact financial flexibility.

2. Exchange Rate: 25% of revenue comes from international operations,
   exposing the company to FX risks.

3. Interest Rate: With rising interest rates, the cost of capital may increase,
   impacting future financing costs.
